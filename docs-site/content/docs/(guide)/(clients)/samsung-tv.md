---
title: Samsung TV
description: The Samsung TV app is punktfunk's and doesn't connect to a Lilynk host.
---

punktfunk's Samsung TV app, [client-tizen](https://github.com/punktfunk/client-tizen), is its
[browser client](/docs/browser-client) packaged for Tizen, for sets from 2024 on (Tizen 8.0). It
speaks punktfunk's protocol and doesn't connect to a Lilynk host. Lilynk has no Samsung TV client.
